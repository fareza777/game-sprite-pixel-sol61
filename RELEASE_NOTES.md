# Vespergrave 3.0.0

The three-trial Cinder Vow slice becomes Vespergrave: The Bell Below, a complete offline portrait descent through 100 floors and ten biomes.

- Rebuilt mage with a human face, hood, shoulder mantle, layered robe, grimoire, carved staff and dedicated casting, dodge and fallen poses.
- Revised enemy heads and connected neck, shoulder and torso masses. All 60 designs were reviewed individually across 540 key poses; the final connectivity report contains no failures.
- 8,400 character frames, three heroes, 60 enemies including ten bosses, nine skills, six equipment slots and 100 seeded item designs.
- Connected procedural room graphs, treasures, hearths, merchants, leveling, skill strengthening, autosave, permanent embers and two endings.
- Eight floor route plans with loops and branches; twelve room layouts with distinct cover, pits, water, machinery, shelves, tombs, roots and open combat space. A floor does not repeat a room layout.
- Ten illustrated, interactive events with resource costs, permanent run boons, ambushes, an empowered champion, a twelve-second hazard trial and a three-wave challenge. Choices, living enemies, timers and rewards survive Continue without paying twice.
- New title, launcher icon, splash, illustrated cinematic, 30 offline Deepgram narration clips, onboarding, menu pages, journal and animated bestiary.
- Full portrait viewport, a small room map and a floating D-pad. Independent fingers operate primary attacks, guard, dodge, skills, healing and interaction.
- Repositioned urns onto the floor contact and aligned flames with their brazier bowls.
- Removed repeated bell ornaments from the walkable floor and bounded enemy atlas caching across biome transitions.
- Signed Android update retains the previous package and certificate.

Verification includes the complete campaign and asset checks, all 8,400 source frames through the shipping renderer, multi-pointer control tests, and an Android instrumentation run with offline audio playback and persistent Continue.

Rate currently stores a private rating on the device and offers project feedback; no store page is published. Long-run difficulty has automated coverage and still benefits from human playtesting.
