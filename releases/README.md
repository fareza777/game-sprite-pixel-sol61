# Vespergrave 3.1.0

[Download the signed Android APK](Vespergrave-3.1.0.apk?raw=true)

- Full portrait offline roguelike: 100 floors, ten biomes, three heroes, sixty enemies and ten bosses.
- A new watchfire menu, three illustrated hero portraits and eight layered opening shots, with 52 offline English voice clips covering the story, lessons, realms, bosses and event choices. Cinematic captions follow sentence cues estimated from each measured clip duration; shots advance when the voice ends.
- Seeded branching floor graphs with loops and 7–11 chambers. Twenty room designs use four floor finishes and ten biome palettes. Real doors can face any cardinal direction; stairs, spiral shafts, lifts and wells have varying positions.
- A spaced battle HUD, hidden empty skill slots, context actions only when useful, and an expanding exploration map.
- Six equipment slots around an animated hero, item grid, filters, sorting, lore, stat comparisons and nine illustrated skill previews.
- Refined native character artwork, 288 four-direction casting poses, 108 spell-effect keys and 360 measured prop contact points, authored and inspected through MCP Aseprite.
- The existing release certificate is retained. Installation over 3.0.0 and a signed cold launch were verified on the Android test emulator. Older 3.0 saves retain their original floor graph until the next floor.
